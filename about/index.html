About Free ZIP Tool

Free ZIP Tool helps you open ZIP archives and create new ones entirely in your web browser. Your files are read on your device with JavaScript; they are never uploaded to a server.

Open-source base

This site is built on unzip-files-local by Or Davidai Bachar, licensed under the MIT License. We kept the MIT LICENSE file and thank Or for the careful ZIP parsing, previews, and repack feature.

Compression library

ZIP reading and writing uses fflate 0.8.2 (MIT, Copyright (c) 2023 Arjun Barrett), vendored in vendor/fflate/ with its own LICENSE.

Part of web-free.site

Free ZIP Tool is one of the free utilities on web-free.site — browser tools with no sign-up and no server-side file access.
